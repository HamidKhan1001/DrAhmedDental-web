Greater palatine nerve

A branch of the maxillary nerve (V2) from the pterygopalatine fossa. It descends through the greater palatine canal with the descending palatine artery and emerges from the greater palatine foramen, palatal to the upper molars.

Open in 3D →

Function

Sensation to the hard palate and the palatal gingiva from the canine backwards; near the canine its area overlaps with that of the nasopalatine nerve.

Clinical relevance

Anaesthetised by a greater palatine block near its foramen for work on the palatal gingiva of the upper premolars and molars. The path shown is schematic.
