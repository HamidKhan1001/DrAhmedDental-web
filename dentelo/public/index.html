Our Services

What We Provide

  • Root Canal

  • Alignment Teeth

  • Cosmetic Teeth

    Whitening, bonding and veneers to brighten and reshape your smile, planned around your natural teeth.

About Us

We Care For Your Dental Health

At Dr. Ahmed Dental Clinic you see the same dentist at every visit. That means unhurried appointments, a treatment plan that fits your life, and a dentist who already knows your history.

We believe you should understand your own teeth. Every consultation can use an interactive 3D model, so before you decide on any treatment we can show you the inside of a molar, where a nerve runs, or exactly what a root canal does.
